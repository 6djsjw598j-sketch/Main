Choose your mount

The right mount for the room

Bring your own or we'll supply a heavy-gauge steel mount rated well above your TV's weight.

Fixed

Slimmest profile, sits almost flush. Best when the TV is at eye level and glare isn't an issue.

Tilting

Angles down to cut glare and improve the view when the TV sits higher, like over a console.

Full-motion

Pulls out, swivels and tilts. Ideal for corners, open-plan rooms and seeing the screen from the kitchen.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does an installation take?

A standard mount takes 45–75 minutes. In-wall cable concealment or masonry adds about 30–60 minutes per TV.

Do I need to buy a mount first?

No. We carry fixed, tilting and full-motion mounts for every size, or we're happy to install one you already have as long as it's rated for your TV.

Can you hide the wires inside the wall?

Yes, in most drywall installs. We use a code-compliant in-wall power and cable kit, so we never run a regular power cord inside the wall. Where in-wall isn't possible (brick, firewalls), we use a slim paintable cover.

Is it safe to mount a TV above a fireplace?

Usually, if the mantel deflects heat. We check the temperature and the manufacturer's guidance first, and recommend a pull-down mount if the screen would sit too high.

What if my wall has metal studs or no studs where I want the TV?

We use hardware rated for metal studs, or a mounting plate that spans to the nearest studs so the TV can still be centered where you want it.

Are you insured? Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. We carry general liability insurance and guarantee our workmanship in writing. If anything we installed isn't right, we come back and fix it.

How do I pay?

No deposit to book. Pay after the job by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer.
